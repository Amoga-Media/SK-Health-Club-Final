Common Questions

FAQ

Do you offer a free trial?+

Yes! We offer a completely free trial session at any of our 3 locations. Just fill in the form above or WhatsApp us to schedule yours.

I'm a complete beginner — is SK Health Club right for me?+

Absolutely. We welcome members at every fitness level. Our trainers are experienced with beginners and will build you a plan that starts where you are and takes you where you want to go.

What's included in the membership?+

All memberships include complete, unrestricted access to all facilities — main gym floor, cardio zone, boxing arena, swimming pool, Yoga & Zumba, group classes, recovery zone, and locker rooms.

Do you have a women's-only zone?+

Yes! We have a dedicated women's zone with separate equipment, and female trainers are available on request. Many of our female members especially love the Vishranwadi location for its women's setup.

Can my partner and I join together?+

We offer special couples membership plans with significant savings. It's one of our most popular offerings — couples who train together stay motivated together. Ask us for details.

Which location is the biggest?+

Our Viman Nagar flagship is the largest at 20,000+ sq ft. It includes all facilities — boxing arena, Olympic swimming pool, Yoga & Zumba Studio, strength floors, cardio zone, recovery room, and women's zone.
