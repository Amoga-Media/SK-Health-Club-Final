Program Overview

Boxing
at SK

SK Health Club's Boxing Arena is the crown jewel of our Viman Nagar flagship. Featuring a full-scale professional boxing ring and multiple bags, it's everything a fighter or fitness enthusiast needs. Whether you're training for competition or just want to destroy stress, our program is coached by Senior Master Trainer Ishaan Sharma—bringing 20 years of combat sports experience.

Boxing is one of the most efficient fat-burning, cardiovascular, and full-body workouts that exist. In one hour, you'll build your core, arms, legs, reflexes, coordination, and mental toughness simultaneously.

Questions

FAQ

Do I need boxing experience to join?+

Zero experience needed. Our beginner sessions cover stance, footwork, basic punches, and defense. Everyone starts somewhere.

What equipment do I need?+

We provide boxing gloves and wraps for trial sessions. Members are encouraged to get their own gloves after joining for hygiene and fit.

Is boxing available at all locations?+

Boxing is available at Yerwada and Viman Nagar. The full professional ring and premium combat setup are exclusive to the Viman Nagar Flagship.
