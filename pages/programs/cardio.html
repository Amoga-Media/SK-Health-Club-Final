Questions

FAQ

What's the difference between HIIT classes and regular cardio?+

HIIT (High-Intensity Interval Training) alternates short bursts of maximum effort with recovery periods. It's shorter but more intense than steady-state cardio.

How many cardio machines are available?+

Our Viman Nagar flagship has 30+ cardio machines. Smaller locations have 12–15 machines each.

Are HIIT classes included in all memberships?+

Yes, all group HIIT classes and cardio floor access are included in all membership plans.
