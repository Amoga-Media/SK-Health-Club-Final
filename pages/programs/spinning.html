Program Overview

Spinning

Our Spinning Studio is a full sensory experience. 20+ Keiser M3i premium bikes, dark room, loud music, and instructors who know how to take you from zero to breakthrough in 45 minutes.

Spin classes at SK are structured around heart-rate zones, interval training, and cadence targets. Every class is a different challenge, every session a different story.

Indoor cycling is one of the most efficient fat-burning cardio workouts available. You control the resistance, so every body type and fitness level can train in the same class.

Is spinning suitable for beginners?+

Yes! You control your own resistance so you can always dial it back. Instructors guide you but you set your intensity.

Do I need cycling shoes?+

We have SPD-compatible pedals and provide cycling shoes to borrow. Members are encouraged to invest in their own over time.
