FAQ

Are group classes included in standard gym memberships?+

Yes, all group Zumba, Yoga, and Abs Batch sessions are fully included across all general SK Health Club membership plans. We also offer the standalone ₹1,999/mo Zumba-only plan for dance fitness enthusiasts.

Do I need prior dance experience for Zumba?+

Not at all! Zumba is designed for everyone to dance, sweat, smile, and repeat. Simply follow the rhythm and energetic guidance of our certified instructors.

Do I need to bring my own yoga mat?+

We provide sanitized premium mats in the studio, but members are always welcome to bring their personal mats if they prefer.
