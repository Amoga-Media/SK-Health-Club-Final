Program Overview

Martial Arts
at SK

Led by recognized grandmaster Master Shifu Naveen Waghile with over four decades of mastery, our martial arts program seamlessly blends centuries-old Eastern traditions with modern athletic conditioning.

Whether practicing the fluid internal power of Tai-Chi & Qi-Gong, mastering the explosive striking of Sanda Kickboxing, or cultivating deep physical conditioning through Traditional Shaolin forms, you develop unbreakable mental composure, formidable reflex speed, and internal resilience.

Questions

FAQ

Can beginners start martial arts?+

Yes! Classes are carefully structured so that absolute beginners learn proper stance, balance, stretching, and fundamental blocks before advancing to sparring or complex forms.

Who teaches the program?+

All sessions are directly overseen by Master Shifu Naveen Waghile, recognized national and international martial arts grandmaster with 40+ years of dedicated practice.

Is martial arts included in my membership?+

Yes, all group martial arts sessions and Tai-Chi classes are fully included across all SK Health Club membership plans.
