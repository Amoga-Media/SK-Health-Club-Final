Program Overview

Intensity With
Intention

CrossFit at SK Health Club is engineered to develop broad, general, and inclusive fitness. We prepare you not just for a specific gym test, but for any physical challenge life throws your way.

Combining barbell cleans, snatches, deadlifts, pull-ups, rowing intervals, and plyometrics, our daily Workouts of the Day (WODs) are scaled to match every individual's fitness level while fostering an electric community energy.

Questions

FAQ

I'm not in shape — is CrossFit too hard for me?+

Every workout is 100% scalable. If a workout calls for a 50kg barbell or pull-ups, our coaches scale it to an empty bar, dumbbells, or ring rows to suit your exact current fitness level.

Will I get injured doing CrossFit?+

Safety and technique always precede intensity. Our coaches verify your movement mechanics and mobility before allowing you to increase weights or speed.

Is CrossFit included in the standard membership?+

Yes, all group CrossFit and WOD sessions are fully included across all SK Health Club membership plans.
