Program Overview

Recovery
at SK

Most gyms ignore recovery. SK Health Club has built an entire zone dedicated to it. Our recovery room at Viman Nagar includes foam rolling stations, mobility equipment, guided stretch zones, and therapeutic tools.

Rohit Lashkare leads our recovery programming — having worked with athletes rehabilitating from injuries and chronic pain for over 20 years, his approach to recovery is backed by deep practical experience.

Recovery is where your gains actually happen. Muscles grow during rest, not during training. Smart recovery means fewer injuries, faster progress, and a body that can keep training for decades.

Benefits of

When you recover better, you can train harder in your next session. Recovery is a performance multiplier.

Structured recovery routines and mobility work improve parasympathetic nervous system function and sleep quality.

Reduced Stress

Guided breathing, stretching and myofascial release lower cortisol levels and reduce the physical symptoms of stress.
