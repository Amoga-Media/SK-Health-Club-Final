Questions

FAQ

How many PT sessions per week do you recommend?+

For beginners and those with specific goals, 3 sessions per week is ideal. For maintenance and refinement, 1–2 sessions work well.

Is personal training included in my membership?+

PT sessions are included in the Premium plan (8 sessions/month). Other plans can purchase PT sessions as add-ons.

Can I choose my trainer?+

Yes. You can choose to work with Ripunjay or Ishaan based on your goals and preferences. Both are exceptional.

What happens in the first session?+

Your first session is a full assessment — body composition, movement quality, goal discussion, and building your initial program. No intense workout until we know exactly where you're starting from.
